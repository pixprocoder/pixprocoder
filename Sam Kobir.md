Background

I started learning to code in 2020 with no formal background in computer science — just curiosity and a lot of late nights. Within a year I was building real applications with React and Node.js, and within two I was delivering paid projects for international clients.

In 2023 I founded **Pixprocoder Studio SRL**, a registered software consultancy in Bucharest. What began as freelance work grew into something with structure: a business account, invoicing, a design contractor, and clients in Australia, the UK, and across Europe.

In 2026 I made a deliberate shift toward **DevOps and cloud engineering** — not abandoning full-stack, but building the other half of the picture. I now own the entire lifecycle of a project: writing the application, containerising it, provisioning the infrastructure with Terraform, wiring up CI/CD pipelines, and monitoring it in production. My full-stack background is the thing that makes me faster at it than someone who only knows infrastructure.
